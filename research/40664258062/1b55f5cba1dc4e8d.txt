COMMENTS
What kind of comments are suitable?
We want your perspective. Probe, analyze, inform. Challenge, advocate, debate. Inspire, entertain, enjoy. Your contributions make our website and on-air programming richer, the conversations more lively and diverse.
What is and isn’t acceptable?
Please keep your submissions relevant to the topic.
Be civil.
When you are writing about legal issues, remember that people are innocent until proven guilty (that may mean using words such as "allegedly").
Feel free to link internally within the ONC site as many times as you would like. As for external web addresses, we allow three links per post.
Avoid lengthy excerpts from other posts. We want to hear your own opinion.
Avoid racist, sexist and offensive language.
Avoid personal attacks and defamatory statements.
Avoid breaking copyright rules (that includes copying and pasting excerpts from other sites without permission and attribution).
Avoid violating someone’s privacy.
Avoid threats or suggesting committing a criminal act.
Avoid insensitive comments regarding the death or injury of private individuals, especially children.
Avoid posting your message in all CAPS (this makes it difficult to read and in online speak is considered yelling).
Avoid posting press releases and commercial promotions.
If you violate any of these rules, your comment will not be posted and your account may be suspended.
How do I comment?
Comments are solicited at the bottom of most stories, blogs and other types of content. In most cases, you need to log in or create an account before commenting. Annotations can be added to video and other data sequences via tools such as SeaTube and Digital Fishers.
What about including personal information in comments?
If sharing more about yourself is relevant, go ahead. However, we do ask that you don’t include personal information in comments, such as phone numbers and e-mail addresses.
How are comments moderated?
All comments to the Ocean Networks Canada websites are moderated. That means that we read them to make sure they adhere to our guidelines. We won’t edit your comments; but to ensure that your comment is not removed, we ask that you follow the submission guidelines to create a thriving conversation.
All published comments are subject to reactive moderation.
What if I see a comment I don’t like?
If you think a comment violates our guidelines; that’s your opportunity to respond. If you feel someone has broken the guidelines; please contact us with the particulars.
How will you use my submissions?
Your submissions may be used elsewhere by ONC which reserves the right to identify you by name and city of residence when using your submissions (i.e. John Doe from Toronto says…).
By submitting your content, you grant us the non-exclusive right to use your content royalty-free.
How will you post my submissions?
ONC will post submissions but cannot guarantee that every contribution will be published. We may also edit the submissions for length, size or clarity.
We reserve the right to identify you by name and city of residence when using your submissions (i.e. John Doe from Vancouver says…).
By submitting your content, you grant us the non-exclusive right to use your content royalty-free.
