New research reveals the hidden diversity of deep-sea siphonophores
New research published in the Zoological Journal of the Linnean Society by a team of scientists from MBARI, the University of Gdańsk, New York University, and Yale University uses museum material, submersible observations, and genomic sequencing to review the diversity of deep-sea Bargmannia siphonophores, revealing two previously unknown species.

New research reveals surprising link in ocean carbon cycle: swimming sea cucumbers
In a new publication in the scientific journal Ecology, Senior Scientist Bruce Robison reports how the swimming sea cucumber Pelagothuria natatrix interrupts the transport of carbon to the abyssal seafloor in the Galápagos Islands, highlighting the important role of marine life in the ocean’s carbon cycle.
