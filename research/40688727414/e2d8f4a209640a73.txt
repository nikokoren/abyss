In the Field: 2026
NOAA Ocean Exploration works with partners to explore previously unknown areas of our ocean, making discoveries of scientific, economic, and cultural value and supporting innovations in exploration tools and capabilities. In 2026, we will lead and support expeditions and fieldwork in the Pacific Ocean, Caribbean Sea, and Lake Michigan.

Status: NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer — In Port: Honolulu, Hawai‘i
NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer is in port in Honolulu, Hawai‘i, following the successful completion of the 2026 American Samoa Mapping + Transit expedition. Our next expedition will take place on October 28-November 16 and will focus on demonstrating, testing, and evaluating emerging and existing technologies such as autonomous underwater vehicles for exploring the ocean.
