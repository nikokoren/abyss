Ocean Networks Canada (ONC) ocean observing networks generate ‘big data’ in the form of high-resolution sensor measurements, video, and underwater sound recordings from the Pacific, Atlantic, and Arctic Oceans. These FAIR (findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable) open data are used by over 70,000 researchers, communities and decision-makers around the world.
