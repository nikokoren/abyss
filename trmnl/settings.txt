MARKUP AND TRANSFORM
Paste full.liquid, half_horizontal.liquid, half_vertical.liquid and
quadrant.liquid into the matching layout tabs; shared.liquid into Shared.
Paste transform.py into the Python serverless transform editor.
Importing the complete abyss-trmnl.zip installs these files together.

Leave Underwater cameras empty to rotate through all four published
cameras. Show QR code defaults to off. Offline cameras keep their last
good image. The 15-minute refresh rotates saved views; production image
processing separately runs every three hours (23 */3 * * * UTC).
