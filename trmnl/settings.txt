ABYSS — TRMNL SETTINGS

Install or update
Download https://nikokoren.github.io/abyss/abyss-trmnl.zip and import the whole
recipe, including settings.yml, transform.py, Shared and all four layouts.
The generated settings.yml is the authoritative import file; this text file
summarizes the settings for manual setup. Re-import to receive new camera options
and the shared About copy. Updating Liquid alone does not install rotation.

Overview
Turn your TRMNL into your personal window into the deep ocean. Explore
hydrothermal vent habitats on Axial Seamount, a methane seep at Southern
Hydrate Ridge, and the Oregon Offshore seafloor. These are periodic research
camera recordings and snapshots, not live streams. NSF's Ocean Observatories
Initiative and the scientists, engineers and crews who maintain it make this
window possible through public investment and open data.

Custom field: Underwater cameras
Key: source_ids
Type: Select; multiple selections allowed; optional
Default: [] (leave every feed deselected)
Options:
- ooi-axial — Axial Seamount, ASHES/Mushroom vent; about 1,540 m
- ooi-international-district — International District/Tiny Towers; about 1,520 m
- ooi-hydrate-ridge — Southern Hydrate Ridge/Einstein's Grotto; about 780 m
- ooi-oregon-offshore — Oregon Offshore seafloor, CAMDSB106; about 580 m
No selections rotates through all published cameras. One selection pins that
camera; several selections rotate through that subset in catalog order.
Offline cameras retain their slot and last good image.

Custom field: Show QR code
Key: show_qr
Type: Boolean; optional
Default: false
Shows a QR overlay in full-screen view only. It opens the exact observation,
with the displayed still and original snapshot or timestamped video link.

Image processing and cadence
Production processing runs every three hours: cron 23 */3 * * * (UTC).
The separate 15-minute TRMNL refresh rotates saved views; it does not start a
new research-image processing job. Full-resolution source colour is preserved;
TRMNL handles device scaling, grayscale and dithering. Required source credit
appears below the device photograph; downloadable images have embedded credit.
Oregon Offshore's strong illumination can reduce contrast on one-bit displays.
Physical-device rendering remains unverified.
