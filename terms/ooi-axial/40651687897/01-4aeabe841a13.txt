Ocean data that drives discovery










The Ocean Observatories Initiative (OOI) is a science-driven ocean observing network that delivers real-time data from more than 900 instruments to address critical science questions regarding the world's oceans.

Funded by the U.S. National Science Foundation to encourage scientific investigation, OOI data are freely available.

Our Mission










OOI collects sustained, long-term observations from across the ocean, providing continuous data on ocean conditions and processes. These measurements capture events such as storms and marine heat waves, as well as seasonal cycles and longer-term trends.

OOI data are advancing our understanding of ocean ecosystems, revealing climate and weather patterns, and increasing access to real-time information for fisheries and coastal communities. These observations can support coastal livelihoods, help communities assess risk, and provide timely information relevant to public safety.

As OOI’s ocean record continues to grow, these data provide new opportunities to ask questions, make discoveries, and deepen our understanding of the ocean.

How to participate















OOI brings data to your doorstep, without ever having to go to sea.



















Opportunities exist to customize OOI data by adding instruments or platforms to the existing infrastructure, proposing additional or modified sampling, participating in cruises, borrowing OOI assets, or simply exploring real-time data to answer scientific questions.
