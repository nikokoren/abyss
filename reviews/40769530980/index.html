How often are animals visible?

Oregon Offshore, about 580 m deep. Seven calendar days, six fixed time slots per day, with no quality or wildlife filtering. Images are original-colour review derivatives; click the original link for source resolution. Repeated sightings can be the same resident animal.

Decoded 42 of 42 target slots. Missing or failed captures must not count as animal-free frames.
